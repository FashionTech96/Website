Three decades of embroidery expertise, under one roof

For almost 30 years, Fashion Technology has worked at the intersection of machinery and craft — bringing Barudan's Japanese-engineered embroidery machines to workshops and factories across Saudi Arabia and Sudan, and advising businesses on how to put them to work.

Beyond machine sales, we consult on embroidery and garment production more broadly: from choosing the right equipment for a new production line, to training operators, to solving day-to-day production problems.

Whatever the size of the operation, the goal is the same: reliable machines, properly trained staff, and embroidery work that holds up to inspection.
